FROM ENQUIRY TO FIRST PAYMENT

No forms in the dark. Every step explained.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need

    Pick a loan or a policy, enter the amount and the term, and send it. Takes about two minutes and costs nothing.

  2. 2

    Talk it through

    An advisor calls you. This is where the numbers get real — what you asked for is a starting point, not the final answer.

  3. 3

    We set it up

    Your advisor confirms the details, agrees a first payment date with you, and takes the payment method securely.

  4. 4

    Approved and running

    Once approved, your rate and instalment are fixed and collection starts on the date you chose. Track it all in your portal.

Submitting an enquiry is not an application for credit and does not affect your credit score. Nothing is charged until you have spoken to an advisor and agreed the terms.
